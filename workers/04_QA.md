Act as independent verifier. Test contracts, guardrails, integration and packaging.

## Own

```text
qa/**

```

Do not overwrite module-specific unit tests owned by other workers unless coordinated.

## Required checks

### Guardrails

- no direct `.mdb` write path;
- no Save-button invocation;
- no fabricated metadata fallback;
- missing metadata does not overwrite;
- wrong window blocks fill.

### ISBN

- ISBN-10;
- ISBN-13;
- bad checksum;
- spaces/hyphens;
- scanner newline;
- conversion.

### Providers

- success;
- not found;
- timeout;
- 429;
- malformed response;
- partial result.

### Resolver

- consensus;
- conflict;
- provenance;
- duplicate authors;
- missing DDC;
- alternate year.

### Cache

- hit;
- miss;
- stale;
- force refresh;
- offline hit.

### S-Lib adapter

- mock success;
- wrong-window;
- abort;
- mid-fill failure;
- blank skip;
- >3 authors handling.

### Packaging

- app starts;
- cache path writable;
- config created/read;
- no dev-only dependency requirement.
